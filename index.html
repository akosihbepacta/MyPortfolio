// About Me

Building websites people actually rely on

I'm a Senior Full-Stack Web Developer with over 8 years of hands-on experience building and managing websites for real clients. My work covers the full lifecycle — from initial development and CMS setup through to troubleshooting, performance optimization, and long-term maintenance.

At Technomancer Inc., I spent more than seven years building and maintaining a wide range of client websites — WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, and custom Laravel and Vue.js applications. I'm just as comfortable working inside a page builder as I am writing server-side PHP or setting up a deployment pipeline.

I care about the things that matter after launch: fast load times, clean code, accessible markup, and websites that keep working reliably. I'm used to working independently and as part of a team, communicating clearly, and juggling multiple projects without losing quality.
